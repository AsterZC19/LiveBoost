Noto Sans JP Regular, Medium and Bold are the unmodified Japanese subset OTFs from
[notofonts/noto-cjk](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk/tree/main/Sans/SubsetOTF/JP),
downloaded on 2026-09-08. These fonts and the existing Noto Sans SC fonts are licensed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see [OFL.txt](OFL.txt).

Japanese rendering prefers Noto Sans JP and falls back to Noto Sans SC for Chinese
player names and profile text. Chinese rendering uses Noto Sans SC.
